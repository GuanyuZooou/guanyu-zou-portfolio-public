Source snapshot 2026-07-16

A browser lab plus structured exports for reusing colour decisions beyond one figure.

Inspect where colour does work

Colour frequency alone does not explain a figure. The workflow separates candidate data colours, structural neutrals and emphasis. The reviewer supplies the figure context and checks suggested roles against the original graphic.

  • Browser Inspect and Transfer implementation

Transfer and export

The browser lab lets the user inspect a figure and review a proposed colour mapping for a new graphic. Structured palette records and CSS variables preserve the choices for reuse. The reviewer checks contrast and the intended interpretation before applying the mapping. In the built-in Transfer example, the result surfaces uncertainties such as the absence of a clear structural neutral. The reviewer can decide whether the suggested mapping is appropriate.
