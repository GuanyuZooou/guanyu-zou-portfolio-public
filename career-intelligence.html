The public demo shows where a match comes from and which points need checking. The local workspace supports drafting in English and Simplified Chinese.

Match experience to the role

The tool separates direct and transferable matches from possible matches, gaps and missing information. Each match keeps its source reference in English and Simplified Chinese. New or edited translations stay out of drafts until reviewed.

Work with your own records locally

The single-user workspace keeps your records on your own machine. You can review each match, its source and the wording before using a draft.

What was tested

All 14 automated tests passed using fictional data. A browser walkthrough also checked saving, reviewing translations and preparing drafts. Hiring outcomes and multi-user use remain open questions.
