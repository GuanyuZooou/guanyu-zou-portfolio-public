01 / RESEARCH QUESTION

Which features should a model use?

In microstructure analysis, the choice of inputs matters. I’d like to explore how to choose measurements based on the material behaviour I want to predict.

I’m drawing on my work in simulation, regression and measurements from 3D images.

02 / STARTING POINT

Start with a simple model

I would choose a specific question, record where the data came from and fit a simple model first. That gives more complex approaches something concrete to beat.

03 / HOW I WOULD TEST IT

Check new cases and physical behaviour

I would keep some cases out of development to see how the model behaves on new inputs. I would also check whether its predictions make physical sense for the intended use.

04 / CURRENT STAGE

Where the work stands

I’m defining the questions and planning how to test them. Training and comparing predictive models are future work.
