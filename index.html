Doctoral research

How does fibre structure affect heat loss, and what can that tell us about insulation design?

I use fibre-scale models to understand how heat moves through the material, then estimate its overall thermal conductivity for furnace heat-loss calculations. I automate model setup and data extraction to compare simulations consistently. A parallel CT study asks how well images capture the structure these calculations depend on.

  • FEM · Model heat transfer through fibres

    I build 3D fibre networks in COMSOL and model how heat moves through the fibres by conduction and between surfaces by radiation.

  • Scientific ML · Estimate thermal conductivity

    I compare and test simpler models that can estimate conductivity without rerunning a full simulation. These estimates can inform furnace heat-loss calculations and insulation design.

  • 3D imaging · Check what CT can measure

    I compare measurements from controlled CT-like volumes with reference structures to see how resolution and sampling affect estimates of porosity, fibre direction and connectivity.

About

I’m Guanyu Zou

I started in polymer materials and experimental research before moving into computational modelling. I like learning how a problem works before deciding what to build.

That curiosity takes me into unfamiliar areas. I try a small experiment, see what holds up and share what I learn. AI helps me try ideas and write code. I decide what to test and what the results support.

PhD viva passed with minor corrections on 20 November 2026. Currently completing thesis corrections.

Making technology useful to others

I’ve worked with experimental researchers on material properties and modelling assumptions. I’ve also helped MSc students work through modelling problems under academic supervision. I designed and ran introductory COMSOL and Mathematica sessions that linked the software to modelling choices.

In teaching labs, I worked with technicians to troubleshoot experiments and gave students feedback on reports. Earlier, I developed analytical methods and contributed to technical reports for industry research projects. A colleague’s feedback helped me refine how Palette Miner transfers colours between figures.

I’m interested in scientific software and engineering roles where I can build tools and help people use them.
