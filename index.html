Doctoral research

About

I’m Guanyu Zou.

My background is in computational materials research at The University of Manchester, where my work spans multiphysics simulation, scientific machine learning and microstructure analysis.

I’m curious about how the same habits apply to unfamiliar problems. I enjoy framing a question, testing ideas against evidence and turning a useful method into something others can use. AI helps me explore and build. I rely on evidence and scientific judgement to decide what holds up.

PhD viva passed with minor corrections on 20 November 2026. Currently completing thesis corrections.
