Doctoral research

How does fibre structure affect heat loss through insulation?

I use fibre-scale simulations to study how heat moves through insulation. I use their results to estimate the material’s thermal conductivity for furnace heat-loss calculations and insulation design. A separate imaging study tests how reliably we can measure the fibre structure.

  • FEM · Model heat transfer through fibres

    I build 3D fibre networks in COMSOL to study conduction through the material and radiation between surfaces.

  • Scientific ML · Estimate thermal conductivity

    I test simpler models that estimate thermal conductivity without rerunning the full simulation.

  • 3D imaging · Test image-based measurements

    I compare measurements from CT-like images with reference values to see how resolution and sampling affect estimates of porosity, fibre direction and connectivity.

How I work

I use these stages to develop models and software that others can use.

  1. Identify

    Define the problem, who it matters to and what a useful result would look like.

    Why I changed the investment study

  2. Investigate

    Review the available evidence and identify which data or assumptions need checking.

    How I derive writing rules

  3. Design

    Choose a model or workflow that fits the question, the data and how the result will be used.

    See how the career tool matches experience

  4. Build

    Build a working version, record the main choices and review any code written with AI assistance.

    Who did what on the investment project

  5. Verify

    Compare results with independent checks or data and document where the method can be relied on.

    See the OpenFOAM checks

  6. Transfer

    Share the method with enough explanation and examples for someone else to use it.

    See how Palette Miner exports a scheme
