DOCTORAL RESEARCH

3D imaging & structural analysis

I developed Python tools to measure fibre structure in controlled CT-like images and tested how resolution and sampling affect the results.

TOOLS & METHODSPython · voxel processing · Otsu segmentation · connected components
FOCUSCT resolution and fibre structure
STATUSResearch case study

01 / RESEARCH QUESTION

What can an image tell us about fibre structure?

CT resolution, sampling and segmentation can each change measurements of fibre structure.

I used controlled image volumes to see which measurements remain useful as those choices change.

02 / WHAT I BUILT

Measure structure with Python

My work

My Python workflow identifies fibre regions in the images and measures their structure.

  • Store the image volumes and processing settings.
  • Measure porosity, fibre orientation and how regions connect.
  • Repeat the measurements as image settings change.
03 / MEASUREMENT CHECKS

Test the measurements against a reference

I compared the image measurements with those of matching reference structures and checked how much each measurement changed with the image settings.

Saved settings make the steps repeatable. Checkpoints let me resume interrupted runs.

04 / WHY IT MATTERS

Choose measurements for a model

The comparisons help assess which image-based measurements could serve as model inputs. A separate modelling step would test how uncertainty in those measurements affects heat-transfer predictions.
