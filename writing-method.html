A scaffold and validator for a source catalogue, evidence ledger and rule artefacts.

Document where a writing rule comes from

A corpus may contain evidence from published examples, author commentary and analyst interpretation. The workflow asks the reviewer to record which kind of evidence supports a proposed rule, preserve exceptions and avoid treating stylistic patterns as universal requirements.

  • Public skill specification
  • Evidence protocol

Hand the method to an editor

The extraction workflow produces a rule set with evidence provenance. The companion Editor applies an editorial method to a new manuscript. Scripted checks can validate the structure of the workspace. A reviewer still assesses inference strength, counterexamples and transfer to another discipline.

  • Python scaffold
  • Structural validator

Verify the handoff files

The scaffold and validator were exercised against the public version. The scaffold created the workspace and refused a repeat write over it, and the validator rejected incomplete evidence records. This checks the structure of the handoff. An author or editor must review whether the proposed rules are supported.
