Method

How I work.

I turn an open question into a testable one, then make the result usable by someone else.

  1. Identify

    Find the decision behind the request. A weak historical signal led me to ask how competing plans could be compared under the same assumptions.

    Historical study and redesign

  2. Investigate

    Trace claims to their sources, check exceptions and distinguish observations from interpretation.

    Rule extraction protocol

  3. Design

    Choose a representation people can inspect. Career Intelligence links each requirement to evidence that a candidate can review.

    Requirement-to-evidence mapping

  4. Build

    Use AI to assist with research and implementation where documented. Keep goals, acceptance criteria and decisions visible to the person doing the work.

    Project contribution record

  5. Verify

    Test a result against a separate standard. For thermal CFD, check an energy balance alongside convergence.

    Verification report and data

  6. Transfer

    Package decisions in a form others can reuse. Palette Miner exports a reviewed mapping for later figures.

    Transfer Lab and exports
