Doctoral research

How can the microstructure of fibrous materials inform reliable heat-transfer predictions?

At The University of Manchester, I approached this question by modelling heat transfer, testing ways to approximate the thermal response and checking which structural features can be measured reliably.

  • FEM · Model the heat transfer

    Use controlled 3D fibre geometries in COMSOL to model heat transfer by conduction and radiation.

  • Scientific ML · Approximate the thermal response

    Compare surrogate models that can be evaluated within engineering calculations. Use grouped validation and held-out tests to assess where the predictions are usable.

  • 3D imaging · Check what can be measured

    Test how reliably voxel images recover porosity, orientation and connectivity by comparing the measurements with reference structures.
