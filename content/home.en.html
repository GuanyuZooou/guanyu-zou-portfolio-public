Doctoral research

How does fibre structure affect heat loss, and what can that tell us about insulation design?

I use fibre-scale models to understand how heat moves through the material, then estimate its overall thermal conductivity for furnace heat-loss calculations. I automate model setup and data extraction to compare simulations consistently. In a separate study, I tested how well CT-like images capture fibre structure.

  • FEM · Model heat transfer through fibres

    I build 3D fibre networks in COMSOL and model how heat moves through the fibres by conduction and between surfaces by radiation.

  • Scientific ML · Estimate thermal conductivity

    I compare and test simpler models that can estimate conductivity without rerunning a full simulation. These estimates can inform furnace heat-loss calculations and insulation design.

  • 3D imaging · Test image-based measurements

    I compare measurements from controlled CT-like volumes with reference structures to see how resolution and sampling affect estimates of porosity, fibre direction and connectivity.

About

I started in polymer materials and experimental research before moving into computational modelling. I like learning how a problem works before deciding what to build.

I enjoy getting to grips with unfamiliar problems. I start with a small experiment and share what I learn. AI helps me try ideas and write code. I decide what to test and what the results support.
