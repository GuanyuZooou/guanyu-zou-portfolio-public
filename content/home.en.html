Doctoral research

How does fibre structure affect heat loss through insulation?

I use fibre-scale simulations to study how heat moves through insulation. I use their results to estimate the material’s thermal conductivity for furnace heat-loss calculations and insulation design. A separate imaging study tests how reliably we can measure the fibre structure.

  • FEM · Model heat transfer through fibres

    I build 3D fibre networks in COMSOL to study conduction through the material and radiation between surfaces.

  • Scientific ML · Estimate thermal conductivity

    I test simpler models that estimate thermal conductivity without rerunning the full simulation.

  • 3D imaging · Test image-based measurements

    I compare measurements from CT-like images with reference values to see how resolution and sampling affect estimates of porosity, fibre direction and connectivity.
