Review a draft or plan a paper

The Editor reads the results and manuscript before suggesting changes. It can help decide what a paper should claim, where each figure belongs and what to revise in a draft. Its public guide lists 14 rules and states the confidence behind each one.

Keep the author in charge of the claim

The rules ask Codex to describe what the study observed before suggesting why it happened. They keep uncertainty visible when the mechanism is unclear. The author decides whether a change in meaning is justified. The effect on manuscript quality remains an open question.
