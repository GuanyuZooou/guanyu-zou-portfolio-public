Source snapshot 2026-11-26

Wall energy imbalance fell from 7.96% to 0.918% after a tighter convergence check on the same coarse configuration.

A converged run still left an energy gap

The study separates a planar slab benchmark with analytical references from a room radiation case with a wall heat-flow budget. In the room case, the coarse fvDOM run met its solver stopping criteria, yet the energy imbalance exceeded the study’s 5% gate.

Tighten the stopping rule and check the budget again

The room run was restarted with the same coarse spatial and angular discretisation, tighter outer enthalpy convergence and a higher iteration cap. The wall balance passed the gate. The separate slab suite checked 21 configurations against analytical or energy references.

What the evidence establishes

The evidence supports numerical verification and consistency checks for the tested configurations. Experimental validation and spatial or angular uncertainty estimates remain outside this study. The transferable practice is to use an independent balance or benchmark alongside residual and stopping criteria.
