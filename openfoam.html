Source version 2026-11-26

On the same coarse room model, continuing the run with a stricter stopping rule cut the wall energy imbalance from 7.96% to 0.918%.

Why check the energy balance?

The workflow checks the mesh and records how the solver converges. It then compares temperatures and heat flows with independent calculations. In the room radiation model, the solver met its stopping criteria while the wall energy imbalance was still above the study’s 5% tolerance.

What changed in the second run

The room run resumed from its previous endpoint on the same mesh, with the same number of radiation directions. It used a stricter convergence threshold for enthalpy and allowed more iterations. The wall energy imbalance then fell below 5%. In a separate set of slab benchmarks, all 21 configurations passed their checks.
