## OpenFOAM Thermal Verification

- **Problem and implementation.** A completed solver run can still leave a physical imbalance. The OpenFOAM Foundation 13 and Python workflow separates mesh and solver checks from analytical comparisons and conservation budgets. The thermal slabs use independent reference solutions. The enclosure case checks wall heat flows. [Verification report](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/openfoam-thermal-verification/blob/38f8fc0f1b574ee8b0b5d41d3880cfa811748ce7/REPORT.md) · [Runner](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/openfoam-thermal-verification/blob/38f8fc0f1b574ee8b0b5d41d3880cfa811748ce7/scripts/run.py)
- **Evidence and scope.** The slab suite documents checks across 21 configurations. On the same coarse enclosure layout, tighter outer enthalpy convergence and a higher iteration cap reduced the relative wall energy imbalance from 7.96% to 0.918%, passing the study’s 5% gate. This is a numerical verification result at the tested discretisation. The report leaves experimental validation and spatial, angular and transient time-step convergence open. [Check results](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/openfoam-thermal-verification/blob/38f8fc0f1b574ee8b0b5d41d3880cfa811748ce7/evidence/data/checks.csv) · [Room endpoints](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/openfoam-thermal-verification/blob/38f8fc0f1b574ee8b0b5d41d3880cfa811748ce7/evidence/data/radiation_endpoints.csv) · [Run provenance](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/openfoam-thermal-verification/blob/38f8fc0f1b574ee8b0b5d41d3880cfa811748ce7/evidence/provenance.json)
- **Value and role.** The transferable practice is to use an independent conservation or benchmark check alongside stopping criteria. Official tutorial inputs and the OpenFOAM runtime are attributed upstream. The portfolio describes the study and its decisions without allocating personal or AI authorship of individual steps. [Reproduction guide](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/openfoam-thermal-verification/blob/38f8fc0f1b574ee8b0b5d41d3880cfa811748ce7/docs/reproduce.md) · [Third-party attribution](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/openfoam-thermal-verification/blob/38f8fc0f1b574ee8b0b5d41d3880cfa811748ce7/THIRD_PARTY.md)

## Career Intelligence Lab

- **Problem and implementation.** A candidate needs to see the link between a job requirement and the evidence behind a draft. A shared JavaScript module classifies mappings as Direct, Transferable, Latent, Gap or Missing. Source identifiers survive English and Simplified Chinese workflows. Pending or edited translations stay out of draft wording until reviewed. A versioned JSON workspace uses revision checks and atomic saves. [Mapping and draft logic](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/career-intelligence-lab/blob/bda2fed44a6452ca3a41fa8d7e9e1783dc672569/site/core.mjs) · [Workspace contract](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/career-intelligence-lab/blob/bda2fed44a6452ca3a41fa8d7e9e1783dc672569/docs/workspace.md) · [Workspace persistence](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/career-intelligence-lab/blob/bda2fed44a6452ca3a41fa8d7e9e1783dc672569/tools/workspace-file.mjs)
- **Evidence and scope.** The single-user local workbench and CLI use the workspace. The public browser demo uses synthetic candidate data. The source contains 14 synthetic workflow tests and a manual Chromium record for translation review, drafts, version history and narrow layouts. The evidence supports workflow behaviour, with hiring outcome studies and multi-user deployment outside its scope. [Workflow tests](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/career-intelligence-lab/blob/bda2fed44a6452ca3a41fa8d7e9e1783dc672569/tests/workflow.test.mjs) · [Validation record](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/career-intelligence-lab/blob/bda2fed44a6452ca3a41fa8d7e9e1783dc672569/docs/validation-and-release.md) · [Public demo](https://guanyuzooou.github.io/career-intelligence-lab/)
- **Value and role.** The workflow makes gaps, source strength and translation decisions reviewable before an application is used. The candidate remains responsible for confirming a match and approving language. The portfolio leaves the split of human and AI development contributions unassigned. [Workspace and review model](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/career-intelligence-lab/blob/bda2fed44a6452ca3a41fa8d7e9e1783dc672569/docs/workspace.md)

## Personal Investment Research

- **Question and negative result.** A rule-based discount-entry approach was profitable in 2 of 14 complete historical out-of-sample quarters and trailed a capital-matched buy-and-hold control in all 14. These retrospective observations belong to the tested cohort and daily fill assumptions. Universe construction, vendor revisions, prior inspection and unknown intraday order limit the inference. [Historical report](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/personal-investment-research/blob/4194da8bee8f9043bcd302abeb6e5faca086913d/stock_research/research_v2/README.md#historical-findings) · [Walk-forward implementation](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/personal-investment-research/blob/4194da8bee8f9043bcd302abeb6e5faca086913d/stock_research/research_v2/validation.py) · [Data sources](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/personal-investment-research/blob/4194da8bee8f9043bcd302abeb6e5faca086913d/stock_research/repro_data/DATA_SOURCES.md)
- **Evidence and scope.** The offline preview screens a versioned catalogue against stated constraints and can return an empty result. It compares an illustrative 60/40 policy, cash, broad-equity buy-and-hold and staged contributions under shared synthetic GBP prices, deposits and costs. Inspectable cash and units ledgers support checks of policy accounting. The deterministic fixture tests the workflow. Real-market and prospective validation remain open. [Constraint screening](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/personal-investment-research/blob/4194da8bee8f9043bcd302abeb6e5faca086913d/stock_research/platform/plan_screen.py) · [Replay](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/personal-investment-research/blob/4194da8bee8f9043bcd302abeb6e5faca086913d/stock_research/platform/compare_plan.py) · [Regression tests](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/personal-investment-research/blob/4194da8bee8f9043bcd302abeb6e5faca086913d/stock_research/tests/test_platform_compare_plan.py)
- **Value and role.** The public development record credits Guanyu with research and portfolio goals, a paper-only boundary, common baselines and retaining the negative result. It credits ChatGPT and Codex with supporting source research, protocol design, implementation and testing. The point for portfolio visitors is controlled comparison and auditable assumptions. [Development and contribution record](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/personal-investment-research/blob/4194da8bee8f9043bcd302abeb6e5faca086913d/stock_research/docs/platform/development-record.md)

## Evidence-Based Scientific Writing Editor

- **Problem and implementation.** Manuscript revision can alter what the evidence supports. The public Codex skill offers audit, plan, revise and draft modes. It separates observed results from interpretation, mechanism and extrapolation. A reference method and catalogue of 14 confidence-labelled rules cover evidence ordering, claim strength, information selection and terminology. [Skill specification](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/evidence-based-scientific-writing-editor/blob/9421eb47414f88fedcc6c0d7bc4a332c2d5ea15b/skills/evidence-based-scientific-writing-editor/SKILL.md) · [Editorial method](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/evidence-based-scientific-writing-editor/blob/9421eb47414f88fedcc6c0d7bc4a332c2d5ea15b/skills/evidence-based-scientific-writing-editor/references/editorial-method.md) · [Rule catalogue](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/evidence-based-scientific-writing-editor/blob/9421eb47414f88fedcc6c0d7bc4a332c2d5ea15b/skills/evidence-based-scientific-writing-editor/references/rule-catalog.csv)
- **Evidence, scope and role.** The public artifacts show what the agent is asked to check. For instance, R08 and R12 support ordering observations before dependent interpretations and retaining uncertainty around mechanisms. The author reviews the evidence and owns any change in scientific meaning. Comparative effects on manuscript quality remain unmeasured in the public repository. The portfolio links to the public method and catalogue. [Pinned public project](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/evidence-based-scientific-writing-editor/tree/9421eb47414f88fedcc6c0d7bc4a332c2d5ea15b)

## Distill Scientific Writing Method

- **Problem and implementation.** A writing pattern is only transferable if its evidence and exceptions are visible. This public Codex skill separates published behaviour, author commentary and evidence of writing ownership. It asks the analyst to record counterexamples, confounders and confidence for each proposed rule. The output contract specifies source dossiers, a rule-evidence matrix and a playbook. [Skill specification](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/distill-scientific-writing-method/blob/d977c5486d41f8ed3246316ff082ca045a81a2ef/skills/distill-scientific-writing-method/SKILL.md) · [Evidence protocol](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/distill-scientific-writing-method/blob/d977c5486d41f8ed3246316ff082ca045a81a2ef/skills/distill-scientific-writing-method/references/evidence-protocol.md) · [Output contract](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/distill-scientific-writing-method/blob/d977c5486d41f8ed3246316ff082ca045a81a2ef/skills/distill-scientific-writing-method/references/output-contract.md)
- **Evidence, scope and role.** A review smoke check used script files matching the public Git blobs. The scaffold created 11 files and an evidence directory, refused to overwrite a populated directory, and the validator rejected the empty scaffold as incomplete. These checks cover workspace structure. A person still evaluates writing ownership, strength of evidence and transfer to a new discipline. The paired Editor applies a reference method after that review. The handoff is a conceptual workflow between the two skills. [Scaffold script](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/distill-scientific-writing-method/blob/d977c5486d41f8ed3246316ff082ca045a81a2ef/skills/distill-scientific-writing-method/scripts/scaffold_extraction.py) · [Validator](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/distill-scientific-writing-method/blob/d977c5486d41f8ed3246316ff082ca045a81a2ef/skills/distill-scientific-writing-method/scripts/validate_extraction.py)

## Top-Journal Palette Miner

- **Problem and implementation.** Extracted colour frequency gives a starting point for reviewing what colour does in a scientific figure. The browser Inspect tab samples raster pixels locally and shows relative shares, candidate roles and contrast against white. Transfer proposes a mapping from a reference figure using the target type, series count and emphasis supplied by the user. The browser emits JSON, CSS and a transfer report. The Python tools support seeded colour clustering and candidate panel detection based on whitespace. [Browser implementation](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/top-journal-palette-miner/blob/4063ff7dfe6ae9e0f3d6e72d4e6623e119f57ef9/docs/index.html) · [Palette analysis code](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/top-journal-palette-miner/blob/4063ff7dfe6ae9e0f3d6e72d4e6623e119f57ef9/skills/top-journal-palette-miner/scripts/mine_palette.py) · [Panel detection code](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/top-journal-palette-miner/blob/4063ff7dfe6ae9e0f3d6e72d4e6623e119f57ef9/skills/top-journal-palette-miner/scripts/analyze_panels.py)
- **Evidence, scope and role.** At this main-branch revision, the public CI passed and the browser inspection and transfer examples were opened during review. The synthetic transfer example generated a mapping with contrast and structural-neutral caveats. Pixel-based roles are heuristic. The researcher supplies scientific salience and checks the final figure, including whether it needs markers or other non-colour cues. The evidence covers the review workflow. Semantic interpretation and accessibility assessment remain human review tasks. [Public CI run](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/top-journal-palette-miner/actions/runs/29539382085) · [Palette Lab](https://guanyuzooou.github.io/top-journal-palette-miner/) · [Accessibility notes](https://github.com/GuanyuZooou/top-journal-palette-miner/blob/4063ff7dfe6ae9e0f3d6e72d4e6623e119f57ef9/skills/top-journal-palette-miner/references/accessibility.md)
