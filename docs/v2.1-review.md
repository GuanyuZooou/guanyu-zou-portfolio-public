# Portfolio v2.1 review

Compare the candidate branch `portfolio/v2.1` with the local v2.0 commit `f6ec4e8`. The live site remains on its current public version.

## Editorial and visual decisions

| Section | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Hero | The heading and SCIENCE × APPLICATION stay prominent. Three asymmetric image tiles identify areas of practice and go straight to their catalogue categories. OpenFOAM’s archived overview, the Palette Miner gallery and the synthetic investment replay are existing public assets. The replay is visibly labelled as synthetic. |
| Selected projects | OpenFOAM, Career Intelligence and Palette Miner share a 16:9 image frame. Each card has one problem, one traceable outcome and its own case study or demo action. Images are contained without cropping plotted axes or interface content. |
| Catalogue | One list per category replaces the filter bar. Desktop and scripts-disabled views show all projects. At widths up to 700 px, native buttons reveal the mobile panels. Research & Engineering starts open, and a direct category link reveals its target. |
| Method | A shared rail connects the steps in two rows on desktop. Mobile uses one vertical sequence. Each stage still has a link to the project or evidence behind it. |
| About | Adds the motivation for applying research habits to unfamiliar questions and frames AI as assistance for exploration and implementation. Scientific judgement remains with the researcher. The viva date and correction status are unchanged. |

## Browser and evidence checks

- Ran 31 page/viewport checks in actual Chromium. All 12 routes at 390 and 1440 px, plus the homepage at 320, 360, 430, 700, 701, 768 and 1024 px. No overflow, image load failures or uncaught page errors. Inspected the matched homepage views, featured cards, mobile catalogue, and desktop and mobile method sequences.
- Axe 4.10.3 reported no automated WCAG 2.0/2.1 A/AA violations across 24 page/viewport checks. Keyboard checks covered the skip link and menu, collapsed links leaving the tab order, Enter and Space, and focus retention when crossing the mobile breakpoint. No screen-reader testing was performed.
- Checked all three featured case study actions. Both public demo endpoints returned HTTP 200. Verified direct category hashes, existing `#skills` and `#tools` aliases, repeated same-hash links, case breadcrumbs and browser Back. With scripts disabled, all 11 project links and the primary navigation remain available.
- The static verifier checked 12 pages, 250 internal references, 43 distinct external destinations, and 15 approved assets. The page metadata and research disclosure were checked. Public case-study bodies and the existing image files match v2.0. The set of external destinations is unchanged.
- The [evidence inventory](project-evidence.md) still supports the revised card copy. The OpenFOAM numbers refer to a numerical conservation check on the same coarse setup. The Career claim describes the public synthetic workflow. The Palette claim describes review and available export formats.

## Review limits

The page measurements and internal walkthrough are useful proxies for finding evidence sooner. The 10-second and 60-second targets have not been measured with independent visitors. Dense plots and interface text still need their full-size case study views on narrow phones. Review the new first-person About paragraph before authorising publication.
